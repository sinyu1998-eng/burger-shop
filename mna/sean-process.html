4. What "done" means

A deliverable is only accepted when all three are true:

  1. It exists in writing — a document or a live URL, not a conversation.
  2. It states what to do next — every finding carries a recommended action.
  3. You can verify it without us — you can open it, click it, or check the number yourself.

If any of the three is missing, it is not done — and it does not count against the timeline.

5. Current blocker on your side

Your domain is showing a Cloudflare error:

Error 1000 — DNS points to prohibited IP

This is a DNS record problem, not a server problem. Two causes are possible:

  • The A record points at an IP that Cloudflare will not proxy (typically an address that already belongs to Cloudflare or a shared host).
  • The domain is added to two Cloudflare accounts, so the zone is in conflict.

Fix: in Cloudflare → DNS, point the record at your real origin IP. If the origin sits behind a tunnel, delete the A record and use a CNAME to the tunnel hostname. Propagation takes about two minutes.

Once that resolves, step 3 can go live. Until then the site cannot be verified, so D3 stays open.
